Selected work · Claims triage

A report nobody wants to read.

Dental offices export an insurance aging report: every open claim, in whatever order the software felt like. Someone has to decide what to chase first.

So the tool reads it for them.

It parses the export, then scores each claim on age, balance, and status, and says why in plain language.

What is left is a worklist.

The claims worth a call today rise to the top, with a draft follow-up letter ready for a person to review and send.
